The First Workshop on AutoResearch for Information Retrieval and Recommender Systems

Co-located with WSDM 2027 · Hong Kong, China · 19 February 2027 · Half-day, in-person

Photo: Diliff, CC BY 3.0

About

The First Workshop on AutoResearch for Information Retrieval and Recommender Systems (AutoR²) will be held as a half-day workshop at the 20th ACM International Conference on Web Search and Data Mining (WSDM 2027) in Hong Kong, China, on February 19, 2027.

Venue: Cordis, Hong Kong, 555 Shanghai Street, Mong Kok, Hong Kong.

AutoR² will bring together researchers and practitioners from IR, RS, automated machine learning, AI agents, self-evolving systems, AI for Science, and industrial machine learning systems to establish a shared foundation and research agenda for AutoResearch. Through AutoR², we aim to (i) bring together academic and industrial researchers to develop a shared understanding of the scope, capabilities, and limitations of AutoResearch for IR & RS; (ii) provide a forum for presenting and debating new methodologies, research agents, infrastructure, benchmarks, evaluation protocols, and industrial experiences; and (iii) identify key challenges and future directions for reliable and effective research automation in IR & RS. Expected outcomes include a clearer conceptualization of AutoResearch for IR & RS, a shared set of open research challenges, stronger connections between academic and industrial efforts, and a community for continued discussion on research automation in IR & RS.

You can expect a half-day program featuring keynote talks, paper presentations with dedicated discussion time, and an interactive roundtable on open research questions and future directions. Please note that AutoR² is an in-person workshop, and accepted papers will be presented onsite.

Topics of Interest

We welcome submissions on, but not limited to, the following themes:

1 Concept and Applicability of AutoResearch for IR/RS

  • Concept and boundaries of AutoResearch for IR/RS
  • Suitable retrieval, ranking, and recommendation problems
  • Academic and industrial AutoResearch settings

2 AutoResearch Methodology for IR/RS

  • Research planning and decision-making for multi-stage IR/RS systems
  • Learning from IR/RS research experience and system evolution
  • Human roles in offline and online research loops

3 AutoResearch-Driven IR/RS Development

  • Data, feature, and representation development
  • Model architecture, objectives, and training optimization
  • Retrieval, ranking, recommendation, and system-level optimization
  • Research-to-deployment and online strategy iteration

4 Infrastructure for AutoResearch in IR/RS

  • Benchmarks and executable IR/RS research environments
  • Offline and online validation environments
  • Safe access to data, compute, and production systems

5 Evaluation of AutoResearch for IR/RS

  • Evaluation of generated IR/RS methods and findings
  • Evaluation of AutoResearch capabilities and efficiency
  • Engineering and scientific value in IR/RS

6 Lessons from IR/RS AutoResearch for Automated Scientific Discovery

  • IR/RS as a computational testbed for AutoResearch
  • From offline–online feedback to scientific validation loops
  • Research experience and continual scientific improvement
