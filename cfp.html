Call for Papers

Submission Guidelines

We invite research, position, demo, and opinion papers through an open call.

  • Length: 4 to 9 pages of main content, excluding references.
  • Template: English, ACM sigconf format (see the ACM guidelines). For LaTeX, use \documentclass[sigconf,anonymous,review]{acmart}.
  • Anonymity: submissions must be anonymized and must not include author information.
  • File format: submissions must be in PDF format only.
  • Submission: via Microsoft CMT, using the link below.

Submit via CMT

Topics of Interest

We welcome submissions on, but not limited to, the following themes:

1 Concept and Applicability

  • Concept and boundaries of AutoResearch
  • Suitable IR & RS research problems
  • Academic and industrial settings

2 AutoResearch Methodology for IR & RS

  • Agent-assisted optimization for specific objectives or research stages
  • End-to-end autonomous research agents
  • Learning from research experience
  • Evolution of AutoResearch systems
  • Human roles in AutoResearch

3 Infrastructure

  • Benchmarks and research environments
  • Offline and online validation mechanisms
  • Infrastructure security and access control

4 Evaluation

  • Evaluation of AutoResearch outcomes
  • Evaluation of AutoResearch systems
  • Engineering and scientific value

5 Lessons for Automated Scientific Discovery

  • IR & RS as a testbed for AutoResearch
  • From offline–online loops to scientific validation loops
  • Research experience and continual research capability

Selection Process

All submissions will be peer-reviewed by the program committee based on their relevance to the workshop and their potential to stimulate discussion. Accepted papers will be presented in person at the workshop, with dedicated time for questions and discussion.

  • Review type: double-blind. Each submission is reviewed by program committee members, and final decisions are made by the workshop chairs.
  • Proceedings: accepted papers will be published in the WSDM 2027 Companion Proceedings Volume. Papers that cannot be presented in person will be excluded from the proceedings.
